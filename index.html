The Anatomy of a Luxury Perfume

Every masterpiece perfume is composed of three harmonious layers.

Top Notes (Opening)

The immediate initial impression upon spraying. Typically sparkling citrus, bergamot, fresh florals, or pink pepper lasting the first 15–30 minutes.

Heart Notes (Body)

The core soul of the fragrance that emerges as top notes fade. Features rich Turkish rose, jasmine, cardamoms, cinnamon, and lavender for 3–5 hours.

Base Notes (Dry Down)

The deepest, richest foundation that anchors the perfume. Includes smoky Agarwood (Oud), warm Amber, Sandalwood, and white musk lasting 12+ hours.
